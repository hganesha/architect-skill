From geometry to presentation.

Each study includes a resolved model, floor plans, elevations, a cut-plane section, a roof plan, an exploded view, and schedules. The revised courtyard study also includes Blender renders. The 3D viewer supports orbit, room selection, floor explosion, roof visibility, and level filtering.

Read the warnings, too.

A passing geometry check does not mean a finished architectural design. Area fit, circulation, daylight, and furniture checks are concept proxies. Layouts use rectangular spaces and flat roofs; furnishings and trees are simple massing. These examples do not establish code, structural, accessibility, or permit compliance.
